# Code of Conduct

## 🌟 Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 📏 Our Standards

### ✅ Positive Behavior
Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Respectful Communication**: Using welcoming and inclusive language
- **Constructive Feedback**: Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- **Graceful Acceptance**: Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- **Community Focus**: Focusing on what is best for the community
- **Empathy**: Showing empathy towards other community members
- **Professional Conduct**: Maintaining professional standards in all interactions

### ❌ Unacceptable Behavior
Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- **Harassment**: Any form of harassment, discrimination, or offensive conduct
- **Inappropriate Content**: Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- **Trolling**: Deliberate trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- **Privacy Violations**: Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- **Unprofessional Conduct**: Any conduct that would be deemed inappropriate in a professional setting
- **Spam**: Excessive self-promotion or irrelevant content

## 🛡️ Our Responsibilities

### Project Maintainers
Project maintainers are responsible for:
- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior
- Removing, editing, or rejecting contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct
- Temporarily or permanently banning contributors for behaviors deemed inappropriate

### Community Members
Community members are expected to:
- Report violations of this Code of Conduct
- Support and encourage positive community interactions
- Respect project maintainer decisions
- Contribute constructively to discussions

## 🌍 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- **Project Spaces**: All project repositories, issues, pull requests, and wikis
- **Community Spaces**: Discord servers, forums, and mailing lists
- **Public Representation**: When representing the project in public spaces
- **Events**: Project-sponsored events and conferences

## 📢 Reporting

### How to Report
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Direct Contact**: Email conduct@kdesktopvirt.dev
2. **Anonymous Reporting**: Use our anonymous reporting form
3. **GitHub**: Create a private security advisory for code-related conduct issues

### What to Include
When reporting, please include:
- **Description**: Detailed description of the incident
- **Context**: When and where it occurred
- **Evidence**: Screenshots, links, or other supporting evidence
- **Impact**: How the behavior affected you or others
- **Witnesses**: Names of any witnesses (if comfortable sharing)

### Response Timeline
- **Acknowledgment**: Within 24 hours
- **Investigation**: Within 7 days
- **Resolution**: Within 14 days
- **Follow-up**: As needed

## ⚖️ Enforcement

### Investigation Process
1. **Review**: Maintainers will review all reports promptly and thoroughly
2. **Investigation**: Gather additional information as needed
3. **Decision**: Determine appropriate response based on severity
4. **Action**: Implement corrective measures
5. **Documentation**: Record incident and resolution

### Possible Consequences
Depending on the severity of the violation:

#### Minor Violations
- **Warning**: Private or public warning
- **Education**: Guidance on appropriate behavior
- **Monitoring**: Increased oversight of contributions

#### Moderate Violations
- **Temporary Ban**: Time-limited exclusion from project spaces
- **Contribution Restrictions**: Limited ability to contribute
- **Public Correction**: Public clarification of expected behavior

#### Severe Violations
- **Permanent Ban**: Permanent exclusion from all project spaces
- **Public Statement**: Public acknowledgment of the violation
- **Legal Action**: In cases involving illegal activity

### Appeals Process
If you believe enforcement action was taken in error:
1. **Appeal**: Email appeals@kdesktopvirt.dev within 30 days
2. **Review**: Independent review by different maintainers
3. **Decision**: Final decision within 14 days
